sync mode · how the panel works

Use it like the pedal: the soft buttons open menus, ◀ ▶ change pages, BACK returns to the preset screen. To edit a value, drag its bar left/right, or turn the knob (drag left/right, scroll, or arrow keys; Shift = ×10). Tapping a list setting steps to the next value (on/off just toggles); hold a multi-option one for a picker to jump straight to any value. Values are read live and set over the firmware's SysEx.

How the panel works

Pages and cell positions come from the firmware's own menu tables (still being checked against the real unit); labels are the firmware's display names. Values are read live (SysEx 0x47) and set with the knob (0x22). On the preset screen the six soft buttons open the menus (white); inside a menu they turn blue and select the cell beside them, as on the pedal. With "Press the pedal's buttons too" on, the soft buttons, BACK, STORE and ▼ ▲ also press the real buttons (SysEx 55 events), and ◀ ▶ / the knob on the preset screen change presets. ◀ ▶ inside menus press the pedal's arrow buttons too, and HIT works as tap (toggle) or hold (TALK). The bars and labels follow the pedal's layout; the pixels are an approximation.

Calibrate parametertell the maintainer what the pedal really shows—

Some parameters still show the raw number because their real unit or scale isn't known yet. To fix one: select it (touch it on the virtual screen — the pedal lands on it too), step the raw value, read what the pedal's own screen shows, and capture it. For a scale (dB, Hz, %…) capture the min, max and one middle point; for a discrete setting (filter type, ON/OFF…) capture every value. Then Copy for Claude and paste the result into the chat.
