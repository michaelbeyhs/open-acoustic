Play Acoustic views

Five touch-screen ways to control a stock Play Acoustic, each for a different job. They share one connection, one preset bar and the same gestures, and they all stay in step with the pedal: change something here or on the unit and every view follows. Every value comes from the pedal; without one the views ask you to connect. Add ?sim to the address to try them against a simulated pedal.

Gestures (the same everywhere)

  • Tap a switch, tile or block: on / off. Hold it: put the effect on the HIT switch (Stage: on while you hold).
  • Double-tap an effect: open all its settings (Board) or switch it (Grid, Voice map).
  • Drag a knob up/right or a bar sideways. Hold still first for fine steps. Double-tap a knob or bar: back to default. Tap a bar's value to type one.
  • Swipe an effect's picture: next / previous style.
  • Preset bar: ◀ ▶ step, hold them to jump 10, tap the name for the list, swipe the name to step. Keys: [ ] and 1–5.

Good to know

  • Read all presets (in the preset list or the connection menu) walks every preset once and keeps their values on this device. After that a preset change needs no reading, and the list shows every preset by name. Reading takes about a second per preset over USB; the menu shows an estimate from your pedal's measured speed.
  • Picking a preset from the list jumps there with a Program Change if the pedal accepts one, otherwise it steps there with the front arrows (the pedal must be on its preset screen).
  • After a STORE (here or on the unit) the library re-reads each preset the next time you visit it. Use Read again if you edited presets elsewhere.
  • The effect send levels (LEAD TO DELAY, UMOD TO REV …) are real per-preset parameters the pedal never shows. The signal order in the chain views comes from them.
  • MIDI CTRL and FILTER are never written: they can lock USB out until a factory reset.
